WHAT IS AN EV?

A Smarter Way to Move

An electric vehicle (EV) uses an electric motor to move instead of relying entirely on a traditional petrol or diesel engine.

Many EVs store electricity in a rechargeable battery. They can help reduce local air pollution because battery-electric vehicles produce no tailpipe emissions while driving.

Types of Electric Vehicles

Different technologies offer different ways to make everyday journeys more efficient.

BEV

Battery Electric Vehicles

These vehicles run using an electric motor powered by a rechargeable battery. They do not have a petrol or diesel engine.

Learn More
HEV

Hybrid Electric Vehicles

Hybrid vehicles combine an electric motor with a conventional engine. Standard hybrids generally recharge their battery internally.

Learn More
PHEV

Plug-in Hybrid Vehicles

Plug-in hybrids combine a battery-powered electric motor with a conventional engine. Their larger battery can be charged externally.

WHY CHOOSE ELECTRIC?

Benefits of Electric Vehicles

Electric mobility offers several advantages for drivers and communities.

No Tailpipe Emissions

Battery-electric vehicles produce no exhaust emissions while driving.

Energy Efficiency

Electric motors typically convert a larger share of their energy into movement.

Running Costs

Charging and maintenance may cost less than fuelling and maintaining some conventional cars.

Quieter Driving

Electric motors generally operate more quietly than conventional engines at low speeds.

Note: Total environmental impact and ownership costs depend on electricity sources, vehicle type, battery production, driving habits and local prices.

How Does EV Charging Work?

Electric vehicles can be charged at home or at public charging stations. Charging time depends on the battery, charger power and vehicle model.

1. Connect

Connect your vehicle to a compatible charging point.

2. Charge

Electricity is stored in the vehicle's battery for driving.

3. Drive

Disconnect safely and continue your journey when charging is complete.
